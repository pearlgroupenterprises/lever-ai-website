Common questions

Do I need to know what I want to build?

No. We can start with your goals and the work you’d like to improve, then explore where AI could help.

Can we explore opportunities across my business?

Yes. The conversation can begin broadly. You bring your business goals and operating knowledge; Lever AI brings the AI perspective. Together, we identify and prioritize where AI could create the most value.

Are solutions customized to my business?

Yes. Lever AI starts with the way your business operates and designs the solution around your workflows, systems, information, people, and priorities.

Do I need to replace my current software?

Usually not. Lever AI looks for practical ways to put AI to work around the systems and processes your business already uses.

Can we start with one specific process?

Yes. If you already know the workflow or AI idea you want to explore, we can focus directly on that opportunity.
